# Community guidelines

Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique behavior and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of client information are not welcome.

Use synthetic examples. Never pressure a contributor to expose a real case or provide private documents. Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community.

These guidelines apply to issues, discussions and pull requests. They do not replace professional duties of confidentiality or the repository's security policy.
